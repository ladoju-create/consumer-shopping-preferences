# Consumer Shopping Preferences Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores consumer shopping behavior and preferences between e-commerce and physical retail environments. Using **Excel** for data preparation, **Tableau** for interactive dashboards, and **Python (Pandas + Matplotlib)** for word cloud visualizations, I analyzed survey-based data to uncover shopping patterns and keyword trends.

---

## Tools Used
- **Excel** – Data cleaning, formatting, and structuring
- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)** – Word cloud generation from consumer review keywords
- **Tableau** – Visual dashboards comparing trends by age, shopping methods, and omnichannel retail

---

## Dataset
Dataset of consumer shopping records generated from a survey of 109 respondents including:
- Demographics (Age, Gender)
- Shopping Channel (Online vs. In-store)
- Omnichannel Retailing
- Technology in Retail
- Free-text Review Keywords

---

## Key Insights
- Demographic Trends: Most respondents were aged 18–34, primarily from Nigeria, the UK, and the US—highlighting the importance of young, digitally-savvy consumers in shaping retail trends.
- Shopping Channel Preference: While in-store shopping remains dominant due to the ability to physically inspect products, a significant number of consumers prefer online or hybrid shopping options.
- Motivations: Online shoppers are driven by convenience, while in-store shoppers value the physical experience and immediacy of purchase.
- Satisfaction Levels: In-store shopping scored higher in satisfaction, while online shopping had moderate ratings—suggesting gaps in the e-commerce experience.

Pain Points:
- Online: High shipping costs and product uncertainty due to lack of physical interaction.
- In-store: Limited store hours, inconvenient locations, and long checkout times.

- Impact of COVID-19: Health and safety concerns accelerated online shopping adoption. Many consumers have continued with digital-first habits even after restrictions were lifted.
- Technology Adoption: Respondents showed interest in technologies like AR try-ons, AI recommendations, and in-store tech (e.g., self-checkouts, smart mirrors), although adoption is still limited.
- Omnichannel Preferences: Integrated services like curbside pickup, click-and-collect, and in-store returns for online purchases were highly appreciated by respondents.

Consumer Suggestions (Word Cloud Highlights):
- In-store: More helpful staff, cleaner stores, shorter queues.
- Online: Lower delivery fees, better return processes, and richer product visuals.
